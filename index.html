About Hoop Dynasty

How a season works
  • 82 games, then the Play-In and a 16-team playoff.
  • Carry 13 to 15 players. 14 is the normal minimum; 13 is allowed for 14 days.
  • Set 5 starters and exactly 240 minutes before each game, or let Auto Rotation do it.
  • Trades and extensions close after Game 41. Trades reopen on draft night.
  • Cap, luxury tax and both aprons restrict signings and trade matching, not games.
  • Players develop only between seasons, driven by age, potential, minutes and production.
